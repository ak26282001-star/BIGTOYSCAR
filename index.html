How buying works here

  1. 1

    Pick a car

    Browse the stock online or walk the lot. Every listing shows the real price and mileage, so there is nothing to haggle out of us first.

  2. 2

    Drive it

    Book a time and the car is fuelled and waiting. Bring your licence. Take it on the roads you actually use, not just round the block.

    Finance, trade-in and registration are handled at one desk. You see the full figure before you sign anything.
