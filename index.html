( The studio )

We don’t sell parts lists. We sit down with you, find the one idea the car should express, and then make every decision answer to it: the depth of the paint, the gap between tyre and arch, the stitch in the seat, the colour of the light at night.

Services

Six disciplines.
One workshop.

  1. 01

    Paint & Wrap

    Full respray in solid, metallic, pearl or satin finishes, or paint-protection and colour-change film laid by hand.

  2. 02

    Wheels & Stance

    Forged and flow-formed wheels, coilovers and air suspension, set up for the look without giving up the drive.

  3. 03

    Performance

    Intake, exhaust, calibration and brakes, specified as a balanced package and verified on the road.

  4. 04

    Interiors

    Retrims in leather, Alcantara and technical fabrics, with contrast stitching and bespoke detailing.

  5. 05

    Lighting

    Signature daytime lights, smoked and sequential lamps, and ambient interior lighting.

  6. 06

    Ceramic Protection

    Multi-stage paint correction and ceramic coating to protect the finish and deepen the gloss.

Process

From first sketch
to final reveal.

  1. I

    Consultation

    We meet you and the car, and talk through how you drive it and what it should say.

  2. II

    Design & quote

    Renders, samples and a fixed, itemised quote. Nothing starts until you sign it off.

  3. III

    The build

    Weekly photo updates from the studio floor. You’re welcome to visit at any stage.

  4. IV

    Handover

    A detailed walk-around, care guidance and a follow-up check after the first few hundred miles.
