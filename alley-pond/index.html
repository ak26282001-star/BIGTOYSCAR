About

A Queens landmark, reimagined

Since the 1950s, Alley Pond has been where Queens learns, practices and falls in love with golf. After a renovation of about $10 million, we reopened in July 2026 with everything you remember and a lot you've never seen.

Once voted one of America's top 100 golf ranges by the Golf Range Association of America, the center welcomes everyone: families out for a fun evening, beginners picking up a club for the first time, and experienced golfers grinding on their numbers.
